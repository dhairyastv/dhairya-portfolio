Inspection AI Summary: mobile options

Four ways to fit the summary above the inspection form on a phone. Each phone works: tap rows, tiles and chips. Question chips jump to the question in the form. Bridge inspection sample data.

First screen answers the decisionOutcome + recommendation + 4 statuses fit before the first scroll. Everything else is one tap away.
No hover, no tiny targetsEvery tap target is at least 44 px. Confidence, reasons and finding details open in bottom sheets, not popovers.
Built for the field16 px body text, high contrast for sunlight, and colour always paired with an icon and a word for glove and glare use.
Tighter AI text limitsRecommendation ≤ 120 characters, reasons ≤ 60, key facts ≤ 18. Longer text goes into the sheet.
