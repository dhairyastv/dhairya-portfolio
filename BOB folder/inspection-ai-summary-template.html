AI Summary Card: universal template

One fixed layout for any industry. Switch the industry to see the same slots filled with different inspections, and turn on the slot overlay to see where each piece of content comes from. Sample data.

How the template works

Fixed for every organization

  • Section order: context, outcome, recommendation, 4 assessment areas, overview, findings, review focus.
  • The 4 assessment areas: Safety, Condition, Compliance, Readiness.
  • Status scales: 3 outcomes, 4 severity levels, 4 colours.
  • Finding layout: severity, question, what was found, evidence, action.

Configurable per organization

  • Labels only: "Readiness" can read "Operability" (nuclear) or "Airworthiness" (aviation). "Critical" can read "Level 1".
  • Work order prefix and link target.

Filled per inspection

  • Everything else: form data and AI-written text.

Slot spec

What goes in each slot, where it comes from, and what to show when there's nothing to say.

SlotSourceContent ruleWhen empty or uncertain
1 · ConfidenceAILevel (High ≥ 85, Medium 60–84, Low < 60) with %. Click shows 2–4 reasons: completeness, evidence, consistency, history.Low: amber pill and the outcome says "Review carefully".
2 · ContextFormAsset ID + name, location, performed by, completed date and time, form name.Show "Not recorded". Never leave a blank.
3 · OutcomeAIOne of: Action required · Follow-up required · No issues found. Plus one sentence on the asset's condition now.Required questions unanswered: outcome can't be "No issues found".
4 · RecommendationAIThe next step: what, by when, and which work order. Must cite the questions it's based on.No findings: "No action needed" + next due date. No citation possible: hide it.
5 · AssessmentAISafety, Condition, Compliance, Readiness. Each: status word, colour from the 4-level scale, one line of reasoning, cited questions.Form has no related questions: grey "Not assessed".
6 · OverviewFormTotal questions, required answered / total, optional answered / total, unanswered, time spent, attachments, work orders created (links).Always shown. Unanswered required questions show in red.
7 · Findings summaryForm AICount per severity: Critical, Major, Minor, Observation. Severity comes from the form's failure codes when present, otherwise the AI classifies it.Zero counts shown faded, not hidden.
8 · Finding itemForm AISeverity · question ID · short title · measured value vs limit, or the inspector note · evidence count · action (work order, fixed on site, or no action).No findings: green "All checks passed" line.
9 · Review focusAI1–3 things the reviewer should check before approving: missing evidence, readings near a limit, findings without action, skipped checks.Always at least one item, even on a clean inspection.
10 · FeedbackSystemHelpful / not helpful, copy, regenerate, "AI-generated" label.Always shown.
