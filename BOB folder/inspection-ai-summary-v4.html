Concept A

Questions anyone asks

  • Section titles are questions with the answer as a coloured tag (No / 3 problems / Yes). A reader can stop after the tags.
  • Plain language by default. The toggle swaps in technical wording and codes. Same facts, two reading levels.
  • "Who needs to do what" gives every reader their own line without needing to know their role.
  • Truck diagram shows where the problem is, for readers who don't know part names.
  • Colour always comes with an icon and a word, so it works for colour-blind readers.

Trade-off

  • Two versions of every AI sentence to generate and check.

Concept B

Road back to service

  • The card tells a story everyone understands: inspected → problems found → repairing → back in service.
  • The progress tracker answers "where are we now?" and "when is it back?" without any jargon.
  • A return-to-service checklist replaces the stats. Work orders and the missing alignment check become steps toward a goal.
  • The card stays useful after day 1. As work orders close, the tracker moves forward.

Trade-off

  • Needs live work order status. For a passed inspection, the tracker collapses to a single "Ready" state.
