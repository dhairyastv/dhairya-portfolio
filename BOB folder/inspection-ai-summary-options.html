Option A

Attention first

  • Keeps the current card's order, but the generic recommendation becomes a red status banner with the go/no-go decision.
  • Follows your brief exactly: Health, then Inspection → Findings → Actions, then Insights, then Confidence.
  • The trace rows show which questions created each work order, and flag findings with no work order.

Best for

  • Supervisors reviewing the whole inspection. Easiest to move to from the current design.

Trade-off

  • Longest of the three. You scroll to reach the work orders.

Option B

Action-centric

  • Work orders come right after the recommendation. Each card shows its status and the findings that triggered it.
  • Findings with no work order appear as an AI suggestion card with a one-click Create button.
  • Health shrinks to 5 scannable tiles. Confidence moves to the bottom with the reasons behind it.

Best for

  • Maintenance planners and dispatchers whose job is the work orders.

Trade-off

  • Less room to explain each health theme. Relies on the tiles plus drill-down.

Option C

Scorecard digest

  • A single verdict ("Not ready to operate") next to a funnel from questions to work orders.
  • Health becomes a one-line stat row in the same style as the existing Execution row.
  • Insights become 3 typed cards (Risk · Pattern · Gap), each with a "Why it matters" line.
  • Confidence is collapsed by default and splits into 4 measured factors.

Best for

  • Dashboards and lists with many inspections, or mobile, where the card is read in seconds.

Trade-off

  • Most compressed. Theme reasoning lives in drill-downs, not on the card.
