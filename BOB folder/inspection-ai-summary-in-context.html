A · Refined card

  • Your layout, cleaned up. The asset, location and inspector row is gone because the page title and the (i) panel already show it.
  • The Overview bullets are removed. They repeated the cards and the findings.
  • Every question reference is one chip ("1.04") in the form's own numbering. Clicking it scrolls to the question below and highlights it.
  • Findings are filterable by severity. Each row shows its work order or "No work order".

B · Compact strip

  • Closed, the summary takes one band: outcome, the recommendation and 4 status chips. The form starts higher on the page.
  • "Show details" opens the 4 areas in one row plus the findings list.
  • Best when reviewers open many inspections a day and only need the verdict.

C · Summary + exceptions

  • Left: the decision (recommendation and 4 areas as compact rows). Right: a numbered list of exceptions to review, each a jump link into the form.
  • Completion becomes one progress line under the header instead of a stat block.
  • Reads like a review checklist: work down the right column, approve at the end.

D · Summary + flags in form

  • The card holds only the decision: recommendation and 4 areas. Findings aren't listed in the card at all.
  • Instead the AI flags each finding right inside the form, under the question it belongs to. Nothing is said twice.
  • "Next finding" steps through the flags. The summary becomes a guide to the form.
